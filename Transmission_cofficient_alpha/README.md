### Angle-Resolved Transmission Probabilities and Effective Transmission Coefficients

This folder contains the Jupyter notebooks used to calculate angle-resolved transmission probabilities and effective transmission coefficients across interfaces between dissimilar materials in both transport directions. The corresponding raw data are also included.

For each acoustic phonon branch \(i\) (\(\mathrm{TA}_1\), \(\mathrm{TA}_2\), and \(\mathrm{LA}\)), the transmission coefficient is

\[
\alpha_i =
1-
\left(
\frac{Z_1\cos\theta_1-Z_2\cos\theta_2}
{Z_1\cos\theta_1+Z_2\cos\theta_2}
\right)^2,
\]

where \(Z_1\) and \(Z_2\) are the acoustic impedances of the two materials, and \(\theta_1\) and \(\theta_2\) are the incidence and refraction angles, respectively.
